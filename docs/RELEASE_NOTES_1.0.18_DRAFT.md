# PrayerClarity 1.0.18

PrayerClarity makes Graveyard Keeper's prayer system easier to understand without changing prayer balance or sermon mechanics.

## Highlights

- clearer pulpit information without revealing the final sermon payout in advance;
- redesigned Technology prayer tooltips with compact Bronze/Silver/Gold tier snapshots;
- content-driven Technology tooltip sizing with viewport safety;
- concrete current-quality prayer item tooltips;
- clearer Temporary Effects descriptions and long-duration display;
- full PrayerClarity-owned localization for all 11 supported interface languages;
- maintenance cleanup that stops PrayerClarity from probing ordinary non-prayer items as possible prayer crafts.

## Compatibility

- Graveyard Keeper 1.407
- BepInEx 5

No prayer balance or gameplay mechanics are changed in this release.
